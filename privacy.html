Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026. For the Moonlight Post app on iPhone and iPad, and this website.

The short version: Moonlight Post collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game doesn't connect to the internet, so it can't send anything anywhere. We never receive any information about you, your device or how you play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in one small file inside its own storage on your device: which windows you've lit, your stamps and the day you earned each one, the letters you've read, and your sound, haptics and motion settings. Only the game reads this file, and we never see it.

  • Deleting the app deletes your progress.
  • Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, run by Apple) may include it, as they do for any app's data.

What the game doesn't have

  • No accounts. You never sign in or tell us who you are.
  • No ads. None, anywhere in the game.
  • No analytics or tracking. We don't measure how you play, and we don't follow you across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs. The game is built with the Godot engine and its own code. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social media kits.
  • No permissions. It never asks for your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts. The only thing it does beyond the screen and speaker is a gentle vibration (haptics), which you can switch off in Settings.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We never see your name, your card or your Apple Account details. Apple only tells the game what has been bought, so it can unlock it on your device. Apple's own privacy policy covers the payment.

Children

Moonlight Post is made for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

These are plain pages, with no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts or fonts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs and stores every visitor's IP address for security purposes, as GitHub explains (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). We don't receive those logs.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you, we never share it, and we delete it after twelve months. You can ask us to show you or delete your messages at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a future version of the game ever needed to collect any data, we would say so here, and on the App Store listing, before that version came out.
