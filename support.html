Support

Dear keeper, here are the questions people ask most. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Contact us

Email: xavier@m18x.com

To help us help you, please tell us your device (for example "iPhone 15"), its iOS version, and the name of the level, which is shown at the top of the screen. We usually reply within three working days.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, in the game's own storage. The game saves as you play, including a delivery you're in the middle of, so you can leave at any time and pick up where you were. Nothing is stored online, and we never receive a copy.

What happens to my progress if I delete the app?

It's deleted with the app. We keep no copy, so we can't restore it for you. If your device was backed up (with iCloud Backup or a computer) before you deleted the game, restoring that backup may bring your progress back.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone or iPad?

The game doesn't sync between devices. Moving to a new device with Apple's Quick Start, or restoring an iCloud or computer backup onto it, normally brings app data along, the game's progress included.

How do I restore a purchase?

Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so you never pay twice for the same thing.

  1. Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
  2. Open Moonlight Post, tap the gear on the map, and tap Restore purchases. The same button is at the foot of the Stationer's, and in Hazel's letter about the Full Postbag.
  3. Your isles and keepsakes unlock again.

If something you bought still doesn't appear, Apple can help at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles every payment, and we never see your payment details.

The animations make me uncomfortable. Can I turn them down?

Yes. Tap the gear on the map or the journey, or pause during a level, and switch on Reduced motion. Carriers then fade away instead of folding and flying, chutes and slots move them in one step, the sky stops twinkling, and stamps settle without bouncing.

Can I turn off the sounds or the vibration?

Yes, in the same Settings: Sounds and Gentle haptics. The game plays exactly the same with both off.

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?
  • Undo steps back one slide. Restart starts the level again, and can itself be undone.
  • Challenge levels (timed, limited slides, sealed and in order) have no undo, but Try again is always free.
  • After three failed tries in a row, Hazel shows you the first slide of a shortest route.
  • The optional challenge levels beside each route are never needed to go on.
Letters open over the postcard. Can they wait?

Yes. In Settings, switch off Open chapters by themselves. New chapters then wait in the letter box, which is the envelope button on the map, until you're ready to read them.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Moonlight Post needs no account and plays fully offline. See our Privacy Policy: the game collects no data at all.
