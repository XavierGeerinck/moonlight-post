How it plays

One more delivery, and then bed

Every night the Moon sends down tiny living wishes: stars that giggle, crescents that say a shy "mm?", teardrops that yawn. Nobody has delivered them for eleven years. Now the keys are yours.

  1. Slide

    Drag a paper carrier across the sorting mat. It turns corners under your finger and gathers every matching wish it passes.

  2. Deliver

    When its wax seal counts down to zero, it folds into a paper bird, flies home and lights a window.

  3. Collect

    Earn up to three stamps per house, and fill each isle's souvenir sheet until its painting comes alive.

Five new rules

Every isle plays a little differently

  • The Lantern QuarterThe basics, then timed, limited-slide, sealed and in-order deliveries.
  • The Harbor of Lost LettersBrass chutes that carry mail one way, and a lighthouse keeper waiting for letters.
  • Clockwork HeightsPostmarks that stamp, and gates that open only for stamped mail.
  • Mushroom HollowSleepy wishes that won't budge until a neighbor goes home.
  • The ObservatoryPaired mail slots, every rule from the isles below, and one letter to the Moon.
